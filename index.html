1

Vibration is sensed

A vibration sensor on the chassis picks up the jolt as a wheel crosses a pothole — the same kind of signal a passenger would feel, just measured.

2

The IMU rules out false alarms

Speed bumps, hard turns, and gear changes shake the vehicle too. The IMU's acceleration signature tells those apart from an actual pothole hit.

3

The ESP32 does the thinking

It timestamps the event, estimates severity from peak acceleration, and decides what the LCD should show right then.

4

The dashboard updates over Wi-Fi

The node hosts its own page on the local network, so anyone connected can watch readings come in live — no cloud round-trip.

5

This layer adds the map and the reports

Detections get pinned to the route, and people can add what the sensor hasn't covered yet — severity is what decides what gets fixed first.
